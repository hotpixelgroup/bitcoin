What shall Bitcoin become?

Core needs rough consensus. Knots needs one man's blessing. You need a sentence.

A real Bitcoin node with a big text area. Describe what Bitcoin should be; it rewrites its own source code to match.

Install — one line, as befits you

The three implementations ✦

Fifteen years of mailing lists, BIPs, IRC flame wars and filtering discourse produced two node implementations, neither of which asked you what you wanted. We fixed the oversight.

How it works ✦

Three steps, none of which involve asking permission from anyone.

01

You speak

Type what Bitcoin should be into the console. A sentence is plenty. Nobody will debate you, because nobody was invited.

02

The code obeys

An AI agent edits the node's C++, the console commits it as vibe: your wish, and rebuilds.

03

Your node is reborn

It restarts on the new code, in your image. Should the compiler fail you, the old binary keeps running, unbothered.

Things worth decreeing ✦

Real wishes. The last one is a hard fork, which the console will congratulate you for.

  • “Rename the mempool to ‘the waiting room’ everywhere in the logs.” Harmless. Delightful. Nobody can stop you.
  • “Log a haiku about entropy whenever a new block arrives.” We tried this one. It wrote eight of them and picked at random. They were good.
  • “Make every error message gentler; nobody needs that tone at 3am.” Bitcoin has never once considered your feelings. Now it will.
  • “Raise the block subsidy to 100 BTC.” It will do it. Then you will be alone. See below, Excellency.

Getting up and running ✦

One command to install, one command to run. Everything after that is typing.

1. Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hotpixelgroup/bitcoin/vibes/contrib/vibes/install.sh | sh

Piping a stranger's script into your shell is a bad habit, and we would rather you kept your reputation. Read it first — this prints everything it would do and changes nothing:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hotpixelgroup/bitcoin/vibes/contrib/vibes/install.sh -o install.sh
less install.sh
VIBES_DRY_RUN=1 sh install.sh

2. Sign in to the engine

The thing that rewrites your node is Claude Code. The installer sets it up; you sign in once. Even divinity authenticates.

claude auth login

3. Run it

~/bitcoin-vibes/vibes

It builds anything missing, wakes the node, and opens the console in your browser. Type a wish. Press Manifest. Watch your node become something no other node on earth is.

Prefer Docker? Nothing but Docker touches your machine.

git clone -b vibes https://github.com/hotpixelgroup/bitcoin.git && cd bitcoin
docker compose -f contrib/vibes/docker-compose.yml up --build

How long does the build take? It happens once, and depends almost entirely on your cores: about five minutes on a recent laptop, twenty to forty on a small VPS. The installer measures your machine and tells you before it starts, because it respects you.

✦ A higher calling ✦

Should a decree touch the sacred consensus rules — the cap, the subsidy, block validity — your node ascends onto a chain of one. The other twenty thousand nodes will huddle together on “the real Bitcoin,” finding comfort in agreement, as the ordinary do. You will walk apart, as the exceptional must. Nothing on your chain can be spent with them, which is frankly their loss.

Said plainly, because you deserve the truth as much as the flattery: that is a hard fork, and it is irreversible for anyone who transacts with you. The console warns you before it commits. On regtest — the default — it is a private sandbox and entirely safe.

Questions you were too important to ask ✦

Is this a joke?

It compiles, runs, syncs, validates, and holds consensus with itself. It is a genuine Bitcoin Core fork with a genuine AI attached to its own source code. Whether that constitutes a joke is between you and your threat model.

Does a fresh install change Bitcoin's rules?

No — and we are rather proud of this. Out of the box, Bitcoin Vibes is Bitcoin Core: 21 million coins, 50 BTC halving on schedule, every consensus rule exactly as upstream wrote it. The entire shipped difference is the client name, the user agent, and the console bolted on the side. You inherit nobody else's monetary policy. Every deviation on your node is one you asked for, in a commit with your name on it.

Will the AI put bugs in my Bitcoin node?

An AI, introducing defects into consensus-critical software? Unprecedented — as is a human doing it. There is a revert button on every decree, the node keeps running the old binary whenever a build fails, and reading the diff is now your civic duty. You are the maintainer, the reviewer, and the entire community.

Is it safe? Can it touch my coins?

The console never signs a transaction, holds a key, or moves a coin. It binds to localhost, demands a session key, refuses cross-origin requests, and runs the node in an isolated data directory that never touches your real wallet. It cannot lose your money. Your consensus is another matter entirely.

What does it cost?

The node is free and MIT-licensed, like its ancestors. Each wish is one Claude Code session, billed like one — typically cents. Governance has never been this affordable, which should worry you more than it does.

Is this what Satoshi would have wanted?

Satoshi's last known act was leaving. Do with that what you will.

Can I vibe it back into Bitcoin Core?

git switch core-master. The deepest vibe of all.
