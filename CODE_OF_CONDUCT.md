# Code of Conduct

This project is rude about Bitcoin Core, rude about Bitcoin Knots, and
relentlessly flattering toward whoever is running the node. That is the joke.

It is not an excuse to be unpleasant to a person.

## The whole thing

- **Be hard on ideas, easy on people.** "This breaks pruning" is a review.
  "You clearly didn't read the code" is not.
- **Assume the other person is competent and busy.** Most disagreements here are
  two people holding different halves of the context.
- **No harassment**, in any of the usual forms — demeaning comments about who
  someone is, sexual attention nobody asked for, deliberate misgendering,
  doxxing, sustained pile-ons. None of it is funny enough to be worth it.
- **Newcomers get patience.** This project exists because Bitcoin's contribution
  culture is famously difficult to enter. Reproducing that here would be a
  spectacular failure of the premise.
- **The sarcasm points outward.** At institutions, at process, at ourselves.
  Never down at somebody trying to learn.

## If someone breaks it

Email **harry@hotpixelgroup.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
private. We may edit or remove comments, close a pull request, or ban an
account, in roughly that order.

If a maintainer is the problem, say so in the same email. It is a short list and
we are aware of the irony.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.
